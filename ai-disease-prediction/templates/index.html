Medical disclaimer

This is an educational demonstration project. Its output is a statistical estimate, not a medical diagnosis, and it cannot replace an examination by a qualified healthcare professional. It never recommends medicines or treatments.

A high percentage means the model is fairly sure within the limited data it learned from. It does not mean you have that condition.

How it works

  1. Select your symptoms

    Search a list of symptoms and tick the ones that apply. Age and gender are optional if the model uses them.

  2. The model compares patterns

    Your selection is turned into numbers and compared with thousands of example records the model was trained on.

  3. Review an estimate, not a verdict

    You see the closest match, other possibilities, which symptoms mattered most, and general information about the condition.

What it can do

Show how a classifier turns symptom data into ranked probabilities, and how such a system is trained, evaluated and deployed.

What it cannot do

Diagnose, rule out serious illness, account for your medical history, or know about conditions outside its short list. Many illnesses share the same symptoms.
